## 1. Business Vision

### Describe Smerfume in one paragraph

Smerfume is an online fragrance marketplace and decant platform that enables customers to discover, compare, and purchase 100% authentic Middle Eastern, designer, luxury, and niche perfumes from trusted sources. The platform aims to make premium fragrances more accessible by offering both full-sized bottles and decants, allowing customers to experience fragrances before investing in full bottles. Smerfume focuses on authenticity, transparent pricing, detailed fragrance information, curated recommendations, and a seamless shopping experience. The platform also provides advanced search and filtering capabilities, educational fragrance content, customer reviews, personalized recommendations, and a secure ordering process, creating a trusted destination for fragrance enthusiasts across India. Smerfume is a gender-inclusive platform, serving men, women, and customers interested in unisex fragrances.

The long-term vision of Smerfume is to become India's most trusted fragrance e-commerce platform by combining authentic products, rich fragrance knowledge, AI-powered recommendations, community-driven reviews, and an intuitive shopping experience. Beyond being an online store, Smerfume aims to build a complete fragrance ecosystem where beginners and enthusiasts alike can confidently explore, learn about, and purchase perfumes that match their preferences and lifestyle.

Smerfume empowers customers to confidently purchase authentic fragrances by combining intelligent fragrance guidance with a seamless shopping experience. Smerfume's mission is to remove uncertainty from fragrance shopping. By combining authentic products, expert fragrance knowledge, intelligent recommendations, and a seamless purchasing experience, Smerfume enables customers to discover the right fragrance and purchase it with complete confidence.

### What makes Smerfume different from competitors?

Smerfume differentiates itself by combining authenticity, affordability, intelligent fragrance discovery, and a customer-first shopping experience into a single platform. Unlike traditional e-commerce marketplaces that primarily focus on selling products, Smerfume is designed to help customers confidently discover fragrances that genuinely match their individual preferences and lifestyle.

The platform offers a carefully curated collection of 100% authentic Middle Eastern, designer, luxury, and niche perfumes, sourced exclusively from trusted distributors and sellers. Customers benefit from competitive pricing, flexible payment options such as Cash on Delivery (COD) and EMI on eligible purchases, and complimentary fragrance samples on qualifying orders (currently above ₹2,500), enabling them to experience new fragrances with every purchase.

One of Smerfume's key differentiators is its advanced fragrance intelligence and search capability. While most online retailers allow customers to filter products by brand, gender, or fragrance family, Smerfume enables users to search perfumes based on individual fragrance notes and, more importantly, the position of those notes within the fragrance pyramid. For example, a customer looking for perfumes where Sandalwood is a dominant Base Note can specifically search for that combination instead of receiving generic recommendations containing sandalwood in any stage of the fragrance.

Beyond conventional product filtering, Smerfume aims to provide one of the most comprehensive fragrance discovery experiences available by allowing customers to browse and search perfumes using multiple attributes, including:

- Brand
- Fragrance family (Woody, Oriental, Fresh, Gourmand, Floral, Aquatic, etc.)
- Individual fragrance notes
- Note placement (Top, Heart, or Base Note)
- Occasion (Office, Daily Wear, Date Night, Wedding, Party, Formal Events, Gym, Travel, etc.)
- Season (Summer, Winter, Monsoon, Spring, Autumn)
- Gender and unisex fragrances
- Perfume concentration (EDT, EDP, Parfum, Extrait, Cologne)
- Longevity
- Projection / Sillage
- Price range
- Bottle size and decant availability
- Newly launched fragrances
- Best sellers
- Trending fragrances
- Staff recommendations
- Customer favourites
- Similar fragrances and alternative recommendations

The platform will also leverage artificial intelligence and customer behaviour to create a highly personalized shopping experience. Planned intelligent features include:

- AI-powered fragrance recommendations based on customer preferences
- Personalized fragrance profiles built from browsing history, purchases, Wishlist activity, and Favourite notes
- "If you like this perfume, you'll also like..." recommendations
- Intelligent decant recommendations before purchasing a full-sized bottle
- Smart fragrance comparison tools highlighting similarities and differences between perfumes
- AI-assisted fragrance discovery for customers who are unsure what to buy
- Personalized homepages and product suggestions based on user interests
- Context-aware recommendations for different occasions, seasons, weather conditions, and budgets

To further build customer trust and engagement, Smerfume intends to offer:

- Verified customer reviews and fragrance impressions
- Detailed scent pyramids with Top, Heart, and Base Note visualizations
- Authenticity guarantee backed by trusted sourcing and Authenticity Certificates available in the View Product section
- Rich educational content explaining fragrance families, notes, concentrations, longevity, and perfume terminology
- Curated fragrance collections and buying guides
- Wishlist management and personal fragrance collections
- Easy perfume comparison tools
- Transparent product information, including batch details (where available), concentration, release year, and perfumer information

### Where do you see Smerfume in 5 years?

Over the next five years, Smerfume aims to become India's most trusted and preferred destination for purchasing authentic fragrances online. The platform aspires to deliver a seamless, personalized shopping experience by combining a curated collection of Middle Eastern, designer, luxury, and niche perfumes with intelligent search, AI-powered recommendations, and expert fragrance insights. Rather than simply helping customers browse products, Smerfume's goal is to give them the confidence to make informed purchasing decisions and complete their journey on the platform. By consistently offering authentic products, competitive pricing, exceptional customer service, flexible payment options, fast and reliable delivery, and an engaging customer experience, Smerfume aims to build lasting customer relationships, encourage repeat purchases, and establish itself as the leading fragrance destination in India.

### What are your biggest business goals?

Smerfume's primary business goal is to build a profitable and trusted fragrance business by making it easier for customers to purchase authentic perfumes with confidence. The platform aims to remove as many barriers as possible that prevent customers from buying fragrances online, while delivering an enjoyable and transparent shopping experience.

To achieve this, Smerfume will focus on:

- Selling only authentic fragrances sourced from trusted suppliers
- Helping customers make confident purchasing decisions by providing accurate product information, intelligent recommendations, and fragrance education
- Reducing the risk of blind purchases by offering decants, discovery sets, and partials wherever possible, allowing customers to experience fragrances before investing in full-sized bottles
- Curating themed discovery sets featuring new launches, best sellers, seasonal collections, or fragrances selected by Smerfume, enabling customers to explore multiple perfumes at an affordable price
- Making premium fragrances more accessible through flexible payment options, including Cash on Delivery (COD), partial COD (where a small advance payment is collected when the order is placed), and EMI on eligible purchases
- Addressing genuine customer needs by continuously introducing practical solutions that simplify fragrance shopping and improve the overall buying experience
- Building long-term customer relationships through trust, excellent service, competitive pricing, and a strong focus on customer satisfaction
- Growing the business sustainably while maintaining healthy profitability, operational efficiency, and a reputation for reliability

Smerfume's objective is to become the first choice for customers looking to buy authentic fragrances online — not by offering the largest catalogue, but by providing practical solutions that help customers purchase with confidence and return for future purchases.

## 2. Target Customers

### Who is your ideal customer?

Smerfume is designed for customers who want to buy fragrances but face uncertainty during the purchasing process. Whether they are buying their first perfume, exploring a new fragrance category, searching for authentic products, looking for affordable ways to experience premium perfumes, or adding to an existing collection, Smerfume provides the tools, guidance, and flexible purchasing options needed to make confident buying decisions.

**Customer Personas:**

- **The Beginner:** Wants to buy their first perfume but doesn't know where to start. Smerfume helps with smart recommendations, fragrance quiz, educational content, discovery sets, decants.
- **The Smart Buyer:** Wants the best value for money. Smerfume helps with competitive pricing, offers, partial bottles, EMI, partial COD.
- **The Enthusiast:** Enjoys trying new fragrances and keeping up with launches. Smerfume helps with new arrivals, curated collections, discovery kits, recommendations.
- **The Collector:** Looks for premium, niche, rare, and discontinued fragrances. Smerfume helps with authentic sourcing, limited editions, collector-focused catalogue.
- **The Gift Shopper:** Needs a fragrance for someone else. Smerfume helps with gift guides, gift sets, occasion-based recommendations.
- **The Loyal Customer:** Returns regularly to buy fragrances. Smerfume helps with personalized recommendations, wishlists, loyalty rewards, reorder suggestions.

### What age groups do you target?

Smerfume primarily targets customers between 18 and 45 years of age, with its core audience falling within the 21–35 age group. This demographic consists of students, young professionals, working executives, entrepreneurs, and fragrance enthusiasts who actively seek authentic perfumes and value informed purchasing decisions.

**Age Group breakdown:**

| Age Group | Segment | Focus |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| 18–24 | Students & Young Professionals | Affordable designer fragrances, decants, trending perfumes, gifting |
| 25–34 | Working Professionals | Daily wear, office fragrances, luxury perfumes, premium decants |
| 35–45 | Executives & Collectors | Luxury, niche, exclusive, and high-end fragrance collections |
| 45+ | Mature Fragrance Enthusiasts | Classic fragrances, premium niche perfumes, gifting |

### Which countries or regions will you serve?

Initially, Smerfume will serve customers across India, offering nationwide delivery through reliable logistics partners. The platform will be architected to support future international expansion, enabling customers from selected countries to purchase authentic fragrances as the business grows. International shipping will be introduced in phases based on customer demand, shipping costs, customs regulations, delivery reliability, and overall commercial viability. Smerfume's long-term vision is to serve fragrance enthusiasts globally while ensuring a transparent, cost-effective, and seamless shopping experience for both domestic and international customers.

### What customer problems are you solving?

Smerfume is designed to address the common challenges customers face when purchasing perfumes online. Many buyers struggle to determine whether a fragrance will suit their personal preferences, often relying on limited product descriptions or generic recommendations. This uncertainty frequently results in disappointing purchases, wasted money, and a lack of confidence when shopping for fragrances.

Another significant concern is product authenticity. Customers are often hesitant to purchase perfumes online due to the prevalence of counterfeit and grey-market products. Smerfume aims to build trust by offering only 100% authentic fragrances sourced from reliable and verified suppliers.

The platform also simplifies fragrance discovery by providing detailed product information, fragrance notes, note placement (Top, Heart, and Base Notes), fragrance families, accords, longevity, projection, concentration, and intelligent search capabilities.

To reduce the risk of purchasing a full-sized bottle without prior experience, Smerfume offers decants, allowing customers to try fragrances before investing in larger bottles. The platform also enhances affordability through competitive pricing, flexible payment options such as Cash on Delivery (COD) and EMI on eligible purchases.

### How should Products, Editions, Variants and Decants relate?

Smerfume organizes its catalogue using a hierarchical product model.

#### 1. Product

A Product represents the core identity of a fragrance. It contains information that remains common regardless of bottle size or edition.

Examples of Product attributes: Brand, Product Name, General Description, Category, Target Gender, Fragrance Family, Brand Story, Product Slug, SEO Information.

Examples: Lattafa Khamrah, Dior Sauvage, Mancera Cedrat Boise.

A Product acts as the foundation for all related Editions.

#### 2. Edition

An Edition represents a specific version of a Product. Many perfumes are released in multiple versions over time, commonly known as Flankers or Editions. Each Edition may have its own fragrance composition, bottle design, concentration, or performance.

Example — Dior Sauvage editions: Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Parfum, Elixir.

Each Edition maintains its own: Fragrance Notes, Top/Heart/Base Notes, Accords, Concentration, Performance, Release Year, Description, Bottle Images, Awards (if applicable).

#### 3. Variant

A Variant represents the actual purchasable inventory item. Customers do not purchase a Product or an Edition; they purchase a specific Variant.

A Variant usually differs by: Bottle Size, Packaging, Limited Edition Packaging, Gift Box, Collector's Packaging.

Example — Dior Sauvage Eau de Parfum available as: 30ml, 60ml, 100ml, 200ml.

Each Variant maintains its own: SKU, Barcode, Cost Price, Selling Price, MRP, Inventory, Weight, Dimensions, Warehouse Stock, Availability.

#### 4. Decants

A Decant represents a smaller quantity prepared from a Variant. Unlike Variants, Decants are not procured directly from manufacturers. They are created internally by transferring perfume from an existing bottle into smaller atomizers. This allows customers to experience a fragrance before investing in a full-sized bottle.

Example — Dior Sauvage Eau de Parfum 100ml available decants: 2ml, 5ml, 10ml, 20ml, 30ml.

Each Decant remains permanently linked to its parent Variant. Whenever a Decant is prepared or sold, the quantity available in the parent Variant is automatically reduced.

This relationship also enables Smerfume to create: Discovery Sets, Curated Sample Collections, Seasonal Sample Kits, Brand Discovery Packs, Custom Sample Bundles.

#### Business Rules

- A Brand can have multiple Products.
- A Product can have one or more Editions.
- An Edition can have one or more Variants.
- Every Variant represents a purchasable inventory item.
- Every Variant has its own SKU, barcode, pricing, and stock.
- A Decant must always be created from a single Variant.
- A Variant may exist without Decants.
- A Decant cannot exist without a parent Variant.
- Selling a Decant automatically reduces the inventory of the linked Variant.
- Discovery Sets and Sample Kits may contain Decants from multiple Variants and multiple Products.

## 4. Customer Journey

### Describe the complete customer journey from landing on the website until after delivery

The Smerfume customer journey should be designed around a simple objective: help the customer find the right fragrance, remove uncertainty, make purchasing easy, and encourage them to return for future purchases.

#### 1. Landing on Smerfume

A customer may arrive through: direct website visit, search engines, social media, advertisements, WhatsApp or other communication channels, referrals, previous purchases or marketing campaigns.

The homepage should immediately communicate trust, authenticity, product variety, offers, and the ability to find the right fragrance.

The customer should be able to quickly:
- search for a specific perfume or brand
- browse categories and collections
- explore new releases and best sellers
- discover decants and discovery sets
- use recommendations or guided discovery
- view ongoing offers and purchasing options

#### 2. Discovery & Product Search

The customer can either search directly for a product or explore based on their requirements. Search and discovery should support: product or brand name, fragrance notes, Top/Heart/Base notes, fragrance family, accords, gender, price range, occasion, season, performance, concentration, product availability, collections and curated recommendations.

The objective is to reduce the time and effort required for the customer to identify products worth considering.

#### 3. Product Evaluation

The product page should provide: fragrance description, brand, edition, concentration, Top/Heart/Base Notes, main accords, performance information, suitable occasions and seasons, available sizes, decant options, partial bottle availability (where applicable), price and applicable offers, customer reviews, authenticity information, delivery information, FAQs.

The system may also suggest: "Not sure about buying a full bottle? Try a 5ml decant first."

#### 4. Comparison & Recommendation

Customers should eventually be able to compare up to three perfumes based on relevant attributes such as: price, notes, accords, performance, concentration, size, customer ratings, suitable occasions, other relevant purchasing factors.

The purpose is to help the customer reach a purchase decision with confidence, not to encourage endless browsing.

#### 5. Selecting the Purchase Format

The customer should be able to choose: full bottle, different bottle sizes, decant, partial bottle, discovery set, gift set, bundle or curated collection.

#### 6. Cart

The cart should clearly display: products selected, quantity, size, price, discounts, applicable offers, shipping charges (if any), free shipping eligibility, estimated total, applicable payment options.

The system may also provide relevant suggestions such as complementary products or an opportunity to add a decant/discovery set. Recommendations should not distract the customer from completing the purchase.

#### 7. Checkout

Smerfume should support both:
- Guest Checkout
- Registered Customer Checkout

The customer provides or confirms: Name, Mobile Number, Email (where required), Delivery Address, Billing Information (where applicable).

OTP verification may be used where required to verify the customer's contact details.

Before placing the order, the customer should receive a clear summary of: products, quantities, discounts, shipping charges, taxes, final payable amount, payment method, delivery estimate, applicable return/cancellation conditions.

#### 8. Payment

Smerfume should support multiple payment methods depending on product, order value, and eligibility. These may include: online payment, UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, wallets, EMI, Cash on Delivery, Partial COD.

For Partial COD, eligible customers may be required to pay approximately 20–30% of the order value at the time of placing the order, with the remaining amount collected through COD upon delivery. The exact percentage and eligibility rules should remain configurable.

#### 9. Order Confirmation

After successful order placement, the customer receives confirmation containing: order number, order summary, payment status, amount paid, remaining amount (if applicable), delivery address, expected delivery timeline, customer support information.

The order should become visible in the customer's account, if they have one.

#### 10. Order Processing & Fulfillment

Internally, Smerfume processes the order through its fulfillment workflow:

> Order Placed → Payment Verified → Inventory Confirmed → Product Picked → Quality/Authenticity Check → Packed → Shipping Label Generated → Handed to Courier → Shipped.

Where appropriate, Smerfume should perform a final product and packaging check before dispatch. Customers should receive tracking information once the order is shipped.

#### 11. Delivery

The customer receives the order at the provided address. For COD or Partial COD orders, the applicable remaining amount is collected at the time of delivery. The customer should be able to track the shipment and receive appropriate delivery notifications.

#### 12. After Delivery

After delivery, Smerfume should encourage the customer to:
- confirm or track order completion
- rate the product and shopping experience
- leave a review
- upload photographs where applicable
- ask questions about the fragrance
- contact customer support if there is an issue
- add the fragrance to their wishlist
- explore complementary or similar fragrances
- purchase a larger bottle after trying a decant
- discover new releases or curated collections

For customers who purchased a decant, Smerfume can eventually provide: "Enjoyed the fragrance? You may now be ready for the full bottle."

#### 13. Repeat Purchase & Customer Relationship

Based on customer behaviour and preferences, Smerfume may provide: personalized recommendations, new-release notifications, back-in-stock notifications, wishlist alerts, reorder reminders, exclusive offers, loyalty rewards, personalized discovery sets, recommendations based on previous purchases.

## 5. Orders & Payments

### Describe the checkout process

#### 1. Cart Review
The customer reviews all selected products, quantities, variants, prices, discounts, and the overall order value.

#### 2. Cart Modification
The customer can select/deselect products, change quantities, or remove products. The cart total should be recalculated whenever the contents change.

#### 3. Proceed to Checkout
Once finalized, the customer proceeds to the Checkout page.

#### 4. Checkout Details
Customer provides or confirms: Customer name (first name and last name captured separately), mobile number, email address (optional), delivery address, billing information (where applicable).

#### 5. Coupon Application
The customer can enter a coupon or promotional code. Smerfume validates against applicable conditions (validity, minimum order value, eligible products, usage limits, customer eligibility). If valid, the discount is applied and the order total is recalculated. If invalid, an appropriate validation message is shown.

#### 6. Final Order Summary
Before payment, the customer sees: selected products and quantities, product/variant prices, applicable discounts, coupon discount, shipping charges, taxes (where applicable), final payable amount, selected payment method.

#### 7. Payment Gateway
The customer is redirected to the configured payment gateway.

#### 8. Payment Gateway Response
Possible outcomes: Approved/Successful, Denied/Failed, Cancelled (where applicable).

#### 9. Order Processing
Smerfume processes the payment result and updates the order and payment status. Successful payment → order confirmation page. Failed/cancelled → appropriate status shown, option to retry.

> **Important Payment Processing Principle:** The customer's redirection back to Smerfume should not, by itself, be considered sufficient confirmation of a successful payment. The Smerfume backend should independently verify the payment status through the payment gateway's authorized response mechanism before treating the payment as successful and finalizing the order.

### Payment Gateways Planned

The backend needs to be built such that any third-party service we use can be toggled; only one payment gateway should be in use at a time. Options being considered:

1. Cashfree
2. Razorpay
3. Shiprocket Payment Gateway
4. Zoho Payment Gateway

### COD Rules

Cash on Delivery (COD) is offered to provide customers with greater flexibility and confidence while purchasing fragrances online.

**COD Eligibility Rules** (configurable, may vary based on):

- Delivery PIN code and courier serviceability
- Order value
- Product category
- Customer purchase history
- Customer trust score (future enhancement)
- Previous Return-to-Origin (RTO) history
- Fraud risk assessment
- Promotional campaigns
- Administrative configuration

**Partial Cash on Delivery (Preferred Model):**

Smerfume intends to implement Partial COD as its preferred COD model. Under this model:
- The customer pays a configurable advance amount (typically 20–30% of the order value) at the time of placing the order.
- A configurable COD handling charge may also be collected during checkout.
- The remaining order amount is collected by the courier partner at the time of delivery.
- The advance payment percentage and COD charges remain configurable through the administration panel.

**Customer Verification for COD:**

Smerfume may perform: mobile number verification through OTP, confirmation via SMS or WhatsApp, address validation, manual verification for selected orders, verification based on previous purchase history.

**COD Restrictions** — Smerfume reserves the right to restrict or disable COD for:

- High-value orders
- Selected premium or limited-edition products
- High-risk delivery locations or blacklisted PIN codes
- Customers with repeated order cancellations
- Customers with repeated RTO orders
- Orders identified as potentially fraudulent

**Failed Delivery & RTO:**

Smerfume may arrange re-delivery, contact the customer, update customer records, restrict COD for repeated misuse, or retain/adjust advance payment according to the cancellation and refund policy.

**Administrative Controls:**

The administration portal shall allow configuration of: COD availability, partial COD percentage, COD handling charges, minimum and maximum COD order values, eligible PIN codes, customer eligibility rules, product-wise COD restrictions, promotional COD campaigns, manual approval requirements. All business rules should be configurable without requiring application code changes.

**Future COD Enhancements:**

- Customer Trust Score
- AI-based fraud detection
- Dynamic COD eligibility
- Behaviour-based risk analysis
- Automated customer categorization
- Dynamic advance payment percentages
- Courier performance analytics
- Machine learning-based RTO prediction

> **Guiding Principle:** The purpose of Smerfume's COD policy is not to discourage Cash on Delivery, but to create a payment process that protects both the customer and the business.

## 6. Inventory & Shipping

### How does inventory flow today?

Currently, Smerfume manages its inventory through Zoho Books, which is used for both billing and inventory management.

**Current inventory flow:**

1. **Purchase/Stock Addition:** A purchase bill is created in Zoho Books. The purchased quantity is added to the corresponding inventory.
2. **Sale/Stock Deduction:** When a product is sold and the bill is generated, the sold quantity is deducted.
3. **Inventory Adjustments:** Made separately for stock movements not covered by normal purchases/sales (gifted/promotional products, samples, damaged/unusable products, lost/missing inventory, other physical stock discrepancies).
4. **Decants and Partial Bottles:** Currently inventory is primarily managed at the product/quantity level. However, Smerfume's business model requires the future inventory system to account for situations where a retail or tester bottle is opened and a portion of its volume is used for decanting. For example, if a 100ml bottle is used to create a 10ml decant, the remaining inventory should be treated as 90ml of usable stock. The system should also be able to identify bottles as: Retail, Tester, Partial, Allocated for Decanting — while maintaining the remaining usable quantity/volume.
5. **Returned Products:** A returned product should not automatically be added back to sellable inventory. It should first be inspected. If suitable, it can be returned to sellable inventory; otherwise classified as damaged, partial, tester, or unsellable stock.
6. **Damaged Stock:** Damaged inventory should be evaluated based on its actual condition. A completely damaged product may become unsellable, whereas a product with only packaging/box damage may still be suitable for sale as a product without its original box, subject to Smerfume's business rules.

**Current Inventory Limitation:**

The current dependency on Zoho Books creates a scalability consideration because integration with Zoho APIs is subject to monthly API usage limits and additional costs when those limits are exceeded. Therefore, Smerfume Version 2.0 should evaluate whether inventory should continue to be managed directly through Zoho Books or whether Smerfume should maintain its own operational inventory system, with Zoho Books being integrated primarily for billing and accounting purposes.

The future inventory system should remain simple and should focus on accurately tracking physical stock, usable quantity/volume, stock condition, stock purpose, and stock movements.

### How should Zoho sync work?

For Version 2.0, Smerfume should evaluate a hybrid integration model in which the Smerfume platform manages its operational inventory and order-related processes, while Zoho Books continues to handle accounting and financial records. The preferred approach is:

- Smerfume maintains the operational inventory and order state in its own database.
- Customer-facing operations (product availability, cart, checkout, order processing, inventory updates) should primarily use Smerfume's own database.
- Relevant transactions (purchases, sales, refunds, inventory adjustments) should be synchronized with Zoho Books.
- Synchronization should preferably happen asynchronously wherever real-time synchronization is not required.
- The system should maintain synchronization status and error logs so that failed Zoho synchronization can be identified and retried.
- Duplicate transactions should be prevented through unique transaction references or idempotency mechanisms.
- Zoho should not become a dependency for the customer to complete a purchase.

The final decision regarding which system acts as the financial and operational source of truth should be finalized during the Technical Design and Integration Design phase.

### Shipping Workflow

Expected workflow:

> Order Confirmed → Inventory Verified → Order Packed → Shipping Label Generated → Shipment Dispatched → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered.

The system should support: order confirmation, inventory verification, order assignment for fulfillment, picking, product and packaging check before dispatch, packing, shipping label and documentation generation, shipment creation with selected courier/provider, tracking, shipment status updates, customer notifications, NDR handling, re-attempts, RTO tracking, RTO inventory update.

The shipping architecture should allow Smerfume to integrate with multiple courier or shipping providers in the future rather than becoming dependent on a single provider.

### Future Warehouse Plans

At present, Smerfume primarily operates with a centralized inventory setup. Future warehouse functionality may include: multiple warehouse locations, warehouse-specific inventory, stock transfers between warehouses, warehouse-specific stock availability, warehouse-wise inventory adjustments, assigning orders to the most appropriate fulfillment location, warehouse-specific picking and packing, warehouse-wise inventory reports, different shipping rules based on warehouse location.

The initial implementation should not unnecessarily complicate the system with multi-warehouse functionality if Smerfume does not require it immediately. However, the database and architecture should avoid making future expansion difficult.

## 8. Technical Architecture

### Performance Expectations

- Normal customer-facing API requests should ideally respond within 150–300ms under normal load.
- Product listing, search, filtering, and category pages should ideally load within 1–2 seconds under normal conditions.
- Checkout and payment-related operations should prioritize reliability and correctness over raw response time.
- Administrative operations and reports may take longer where complex calculations or large datasets are involved.
- Product search and filtering should remain fast as the catalogue grows, with appropriate database indexing, caching, and search optimization.
- Frequently accessed information should be cached where appropriate.
- Time-consuming operations (email notifications, synchronization with external systems, report generation, image processing, background tasks) should preferably be processed asynchronously.
- The architecture should support horizontal scaling.
- Performance should be monitored continuously through application metrics, logs, database monitoring, and infrastructure monitoring.
- Performance targets should be validated through load testing and stress testing before production launch.

### Expected Traffic

Initial planning assumptions:

- 2,000–20,000 monthly active visitors during the initial phase.
- Ability to handle 500–1,000 concurrent users during normal promotional peaks.
- Architecture should allow scaling beyond these numbers without requiring a fundamental redesign.

These numbers should be treated as initial planning assumptions, not hard limits. Actual traffic should be monitored after launch and infrastructure capacity adjusted accordingly.

### Cloud Provider Preference

AWS is the preferred option based on its maturity, scalability, and suitability for an e-commerce platform.

Planned cloud services:

- Application hosting (Cloudflare)
- Managed PostgreSQL database
- Object/file storage (AWS S3)
- CDN
- Caching (Redis)
- Background processing (using Celery and Celery Beat)
- Monitoring and logging (ELK or a better tech stack)
- Secrets and configuration management (AWS)
- Automated backups
- Disaster recovery

The application should avoid unnecessary dependence on proprietary services where doing so would make future migration significantly difficult.

### Backup Strategy

- Automated PostgreSQL database backups
- Point-in-time recovery where supported
- Regular backup of product images and other important media
- Backup of important application and infrastructure configuration
- Appropriate retention policies for daily, weekly, and monthly backups
- Backup encryption
- Separation of production data and backup storage
- Regular verification that backups can actually be restored
- Disaster recovery procedures for major infrastructure failures

Initial targets:
- **RPO:** maximum acceptable data loss of approximately 15–60 minutes for critical transactional data.
- **RTO:** critical services should ideally be restored within 2–4 hours after a major infrastructure failure.

These targets should be reviewed against actual cost and complexity.

### Security Expectations

**Application Security:** Secure authentication and authorization, RBAC for administrators, proper session management, secure password storage, OTP-based verification where required, protection against SQL injection/XSS/CSRF/unauthorized access, input validation and sanitization, rate limiting for sensitive APIs, protection against brute-force and automated attacks.

**Data Security:** Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest where appropriate, secure management of API keys/passwords/tokens, no sensitive credentials in source code, proper database access controls, regular backup encryption.

**Payment Security:** Smerfume should avoid storing sensitive payment card information directly. Payment processing should be handled by established payment gateway providers. Payment status should be verified through server-side verification and/or gateway webhooks, not solely on browser redirection.

**Admin Security:** Role-based permissions, audit logging, strong authentication, optional/required MFA for privileged users, session timeout, monitoring of sensitive administrative actions, restrictions on high-risk operations.

**Infrastructure Security:** Network-level access controls, firewall/security-group rules, secure database access, restricted administrative access, secrets management, regular dependency and vulnerability scanning, centralized logging and monitoring, alerts for critical events.

**Auditability:** Important business and administrative actions should be recorded in an audit trail (inventory adjustments, refunds, order cancellations, pricing changes, product changes, permission changes, customer account restrictions, financial/accounting synchronization, other high-impact administrative operations). The audit trail should capture who, what, when, and where applicable the reason.

## 9. Database

### Important Master Tables

**Product & Catalogue Masters:** Brand, Category, Product, Product Edition, Product Variant, Product Type, Fragrance Note, Note Category/Note Position (Top/Heart/Base), Product-Note Mapping, Fragrance Family/Olfactory Family, Gender, Concentration, Size/Volume, Packaging Type, SKU/Barcode information.

**Customer & Account Masters:** Customer, Customer Address, Customer Contact Information, Customer Type/Segment, Customer Trust/Risk Information, Customer Preferences (where applicable).

**Inventory Masters:** Warehouse/Location, Inventory Item/Stock Unit, Stock Type, Stock Condition, Inventory Adjustment Reason, Unit of Measurement.

**Commercial Masters:** Tax/GST configuration, HSN, Price List, Coupon/Discount Rules, Payment Methods, Shipping Providers, Shipping Zones/Serviceable PIN Codes.

**System & Administration Masters:** Admin User, Roles, Permissions, System Configuration, Notification Templates, Business Rules.

The exact normalization and table structure should be finalized during the Database Design phase.

### Important Transaction Tables

**Sales & Order Transactions:** Order, Order Item, Order Status History, Invoice, Invoice Item, Payment, Payment Transaction, Refund, Cancellation.

**Inventory Transactions:** Purchase, Purchase Item, Inventory Movement/Ledger, Inventory Adjustment, Stock Allocation, Stock Transfer, Decanting/Volume Adjustment, Inventory Inspection/Condition Change.

The inventory ledger should maintain a traceable history of stock movements rather than relying only on the current quantity stored against a product.

**Shipping Transactions:** Shipment, Shipment Item, Shipment Status History, Tracking Event, Delivery Attempt/NDR, Return-to-Origin (RTO).

**Customer & Post-Sale Transactions:** Return Request, Replacement, Customer Claim, Customer Support Request, Customer Review/Rating.

**Promotional & Marketing Transactions:** Coupon Usage, Promotional Gift/Sample Allocation, Campaign, Campaign/Product Mapping, Customer Offer/Discount Usage.

**Integration Transactions:** Zoho Synchronization Record, Payment Gateway Transaction, Shipping Provider Transaction, Integration Error/Retry Log.

### Relationships

- Brand → Product → Edition → Variant
- Product/Edition → Notes (with Top/Heart/Base position)
- Customer → Orders → Order Items → Product Variant
- Order → Payment (supports prepaid, partial COD, COD balance, failed payment, refund, multiple payment attempts)
- Order → Shipment (may result in one or more shipment records)
- Product Variant → Inventory (supporting physical stock, remaining volume, stock condition, intended use; volume-based stock for decanting and partial bottles)
- Inventory → Inventory Movements (every significant inventory change has a corresponding movement/transaction record)
- Order → Invoice → Payment → Shipment (related but independent)
- Order → Return/Replacement/Refund (reference original order and relevant order item)

Example inventory movement records: Purchase +100ml, Decanting -10ml, Gift -1 unit, Damage -1 unit, Return +1 unit, Sale -1 unit.

### Reporting Requirements

The database should support operational, financial, inventory, customer, and business reporting without requiring expensive queries against highly transactional tables wherever possible.

Includes: Sales & Revenue Reports, Inventory Reports, Order & Fulfillment Reports, Payment & COD Reports, Customer Reports, Product & Discovery Reports.

Future discovery/conversion reports may include: most viewed products, most searched fragrances, popular fragrance notes, Top/Heart/Base note searches, search-to-product-view conversion, product-view-to-cart conversion, cart-to-purchase conversion, compare feature usage, discovery set performance, decant-to-full-bottle conversion, products frequently discovered but not purchased.

### Database Design Principle

- Transactional data should be normalized appropriately.
- Frequently queried fields should be properly indexed.
- Inventory changes should be auditable.
- Financial transactions should not be deleted casually.
- Historical order and invoice information should remain immutable where appropriate.
- Soft deletion should be considered for master data where historical references must be preserved.
- Foreign-key relationships should enforce data integrity.
- Reporting requirements should be considered while designing transactional tables.
- The design should support future growth without requiring a fundamental database redesign.

## 10. Integrations

Smerfume will use selected third-party services for accounting, billing, shipping, payments, communication, and analytics. Integrations should be designed in a modular manner so that individual providers can be replaced or additional providers can be added in the future.

### Zoho

Zoho Books is currently used for billing, accounting, and inventory management. For Version 2.0, Zoho is expected to continue being used primarily for billing and accounting. Inventory integration may initially be retained if it is technically and commercially practical.

Due to Zoho API usage limitations and the additional cost associated with higher API usage, Smerfume should evaluate whether operational inventory should eventually be managed within its own system.

The preferred long-term approach may be: Smerfume → Operational Orders & Inventory; Zoho → Billing & Accounting.

The integration should support (where required): invoice synchronization, sales transaction synchronization, purchase information, refund and cancellation information, inventory synchronization (if retained), payment/accounting reconciliation, error logging and retry mechanisms, prevention of duplicate transactions.

Zoho should not become a dependency for customer-facing operations.

### Shipping — Delhivery / Shiprocket

Smerfume intends to initially use Delhivery as its primary shipping provider. Shiprocket may be introduced later as a secondary option or shipping aggregation platform.

The shipping integration should support: shipment creation, AWB/tracking number generation, shipping label generation, shipment tracking, delivery status updates, NDR management, delivery re-attempts, RTO tracking, shipment cancellation (where supported), shipping cost information.

The shipping integration should preferably be implemented through an internal abstraction layer.

### Payment Gateway

Smerfume is currently considering Razorpay and Cashfree. The final provider will be selected based on: transaction charges, settlement terms, payment success rate, reliability, supported payment methods, refund capabilities, API quality and documentation, integration complexity, checkout experience, overall cost.

Smerfume should not store sensitive card/payment credentials within its own database.

Payment status should be verified securely through server-side verification and/or gateway webhooks.

### SMS

The SMS provider has not yet been finalized. Selection will be based on: cost, reliability, delivery speed, OTP support, transactional SMS support, API reliability, compliance requirements in India, scalability, ease of integration.

SMS may be used for: OTP verification, order confirmation, payment confirmation, shipment updates, delivery updates, important customer notifications.

The SMS integration should be implemented through an internal notification layer so that the provider can be changed in the future without modifying the core business logic.

### Email

Smerfume is considering AWS email services for transactional and other business-related email communication.

Email functionality may include: order confirmation, payment confirmation, invoice-related communication, shipping updates, delivery confirmation, cancellation and refund notifications, customer account communication, promotional communication (where applicable).

### Analytics

During the initial phase, Smerfume intends to use Zoho Reports/Zoho Analytics where practical for business reporting and analysis. However, the Smerfume database should retain structured and reliable transactional data so that analytics can be migrated to another platform if required.

### Internal APIs

Smerfume may develop internal APIs for business-specific functionality.

One planned internal API is the **Packaging Recommendation API**.

**Purpose:** determine the most suitable packaging/box for an order based on the products being packed and the packaging options available.

**Basic flow:** Order → Product Dimensions & Quantity → Available Packaging Options → Fit Calculation → Recommended Packaging.

The API may consider: product dimensions, product quantity, box dimensions, required protective space, packaging material requirements, product fragility, maximum permissible box utilization.

The initial implementation will be rule-based and deterministic — not AI or machine learning.

The warehouse team should still have the ability to override the recommendation where necessary.

### Integration Architecture Principle

Third-party integrations should preferably be isolated behind internal service interfaces (Payment Service, Shipping Service, Notification Service, Accounting Service, Analytics Service, Packaging Recommendation API). This allows Smerfume to change providers without requiring major changes to the core application. The objective is to keep Smerfume's core business logic independent of any single third-party provider.

## Appendix — Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy

### Purpose

At Smerfume, customer satisfaction and trust are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to delivering 100% authentic fragrances in excellent condition while maintaining fair business practices for both our customers and the company.

Since perfumes and fragrance products are personal-use items, they cannot be resold once opened or used. Therefore, this policy has been designed to protect product integrity, ensure hygiene standards, and provide fair resolutions for genuine issues.

### 1. Return Eligibility

A return or replacement request may be considered under the following circumstances:

- The wrong product was delivered
- The wrong variant or bottle size was delivered
- The product was damaged during transit
- The product has a manufacturing defect (e.g. defective sprayer or broken bottle)
- The shipment contains missing items
- The customer received a product different from what was ordered

Eligible requests must be reported within 1 calendar day of successful delivery.

### 2. Products Eligible for Return

Products will only be considered for return if they are:

- Unused, unopened, in their original packaging
- Returned with all original accessories, gifts, labels, seals, and packaging materials
- In the same condition in which they were delivered

### 3. Products Not Eligible for Return

(Unless delivered damaged, defective, or incorrect):

- Opened perfume bottles
- Used perfumes
- Decants
- Discovery Sets
- Complimentary samples
- Free gifts
- Partial bottles
- Products damaged due to misuse, improper storage, or accidental damage after delivery

### 4. Fragrance Preference

Returns or exchanges will not be accepted for reasons such as:

- "I didn't like the fragrance."
- "The perfume is stronger or lighter than expected."
- "It doesn't suit my taste."
- "I expected a different smell."

Customers unsure about purchasing a full bottle are encouraged to explore Decants or Discovery Sets before investing in larger bottles.

### 5. Damaged, Leaking or Incorrect Deliveries

Customer should contact Smerfume within 24 hours of delivery. May be required to provide: order number, clear photographs, an uninterrupted unboxing video recorded from the moment the sealed package is opened until all contents are fully inspected.

### 6. Unboxing Video Requirement

For claims involving transit damage, missing products, leakage, incorrect items, incorrect quantities, or tampered packaging, an uninterrupted unboxing video may be required. Failure to provide sufficient evidence may result in the claim being declined.

### 7. Authenticity Guarantee

Smerfume guarantees that every product sold is 100% authentic and sourced from trusted and verified distributors or suppliers.

### 8. Refund Policy

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method wherever possible. Refunds may be initiated after:
- Returned products have been received and inspected
- The claim has been verified
- The return is approved by Smerfume

Refund processing timelines generally completed within 5–10 business days after approval. Shipping charges, COD handling charges, and convenience fees may not be refundable unless the error occurred due to Smerfume.

### 9. Replacement Policy

Where applicable, Smerfume will prioritize replacement over refund if replacement stock is available. If replacement inventory is unavailable, an appropriate refund or store credit may be offered.

### 10. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation before the order has been packed or dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

### 11. Failed Delivery & RTO

If shipment cannot be delivered due to: customer refuses delivery, customer unavailable despite reasonable attempts, incorrect/incomplete delivery address, customer fails to respond to courier communication.

Repeated failed deliveries or order refusals may result in: restriction of Cash on Delivery, requirement of prepaid orders for future purchases, temporary suspension of certain payment options.

### 12. Fraud Prevention

Smerfume reserves the right to investigate any claim that appears fraudulent, repetitive, unsupported by sufficient evidence, inconsistent with order history, or intended to misuse the return or refund process. Fraudulent claims may result in rejection and restriction of future purchasing privileges.

### 13. Courier Delays

Estimated delivery timelines may occasionally be affected by factors beyond Smerfume's control. Such delays do not automatically qualify for refunds or cancellations.

### 14. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are requested to:
- Provide accurate delivery information
- Inspect the package immediately upon delivery
- Record an unboxing video before opening the package
- Report any issue promptly
- Preserve the original packaging until the issue is resolved

### 15. Our Commitment

Smerfume will work diligently to provide an appropriate resolution through replacement, refund, or any other suitable remedy for every genuine issue.

> **Guiding Principle:** Smerfume believes that trust is built through fairness, transparency, and accountability. Our Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy is designed to protect genuine customers while safeguarding the business from misuse.
